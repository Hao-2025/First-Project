Fried rice Trivia

When did Fried rice first appear in the world?

Fried rice originated during the Sui Dynasty (589–618 AD) in Yangzhou, Jiangsu Province, China. The original idea wasn't to create a fancy dish, but to avoid food waste: stir-frying leftover rice from the day before with whatever ingredients were on hand. (Source: Wikipedia)
